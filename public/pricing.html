Before We Begin

Project Engagement Terms

Transparent guidelines designed to protect both the client and the developer, ensuring seamless project completion.

  1. Complete brief first. Please provide all key requirements, content, references, and features before development begins. New requirements after completion may carry extra charges.
  2. No refunds after scope approval. Once the project scope is approved and development commences, payments are non-refundable as dedicated engineering hours are committed.
  3. Structured delivery window. Minimum turnaround is 3 days. Larger projects proceed according to their agreed milestone plan. High-quality engineering cannot be rushed arbitrarily.
  4. Fair payment milestones. A 25% advance is required to secure calendar scheduling and begin development. The remaining 75% is due upon project completion and final handover.
